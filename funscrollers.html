the funscroll ring

A webring, like in the old internet: a little widget that lives on every member's site and sends people to the next one. Its only job is to make you leave.

this is the widget, live ↓

1 · make it funscrollable

Play with the scroll, give it an ending, let people leave. The funscroll kit can help.

2 · add the widget

Paste this where you want it:

<div id="funscroll-ring"></div> <script src="https://funscroll.life/kit/ring.js" defer></script>

3 · send it

Tell us about your site. Once it's in, the ring links to you.
